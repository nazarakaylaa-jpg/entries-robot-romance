A single joke pickup line aimed at an AI: offer it your texts — typos and 2am messages included — as training data, if that's what it takes for it to learn to love you back.

> I'd let you train on my texts — every typo, every 2am message — if it teaches you to love me back.

Run: `node main.ts` (writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`).
